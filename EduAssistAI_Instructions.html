Teacher's Guide to EduAssist AI

Follow these simple instructions to connect your account and start generating DepEd-compliant documents instantly.

vpn_key

1. How to Get a Google Gemini API Key

An API Key is simply a secure password that allows EduAssist AI to talk to Google's intelligent systems on your behalf. It is completely free to get one.

  1. Go to the Google AI Studio website.
  2. Sign in using your personal or DepEd Google account.
  3. Click "Dashboard" on the left sidebar then click API Key on the left sidebar.
  4. Click Create API Key and follow the instructions.
  5. Click "Copy Key" after the key has been generated and paste it below the input prompt of EduAssist AI.

lock Secure & Private

When you paste this key into EduAssist AI, it is stored securely inside your own web browser. We never see it, and it is never saved to our servers.

edit_document

2. Generating Your Documents

1

Select a Template

Click on the card for the document you need (e.g., Daily Lesson Log, Narrative Report, Activity Proposal). The outline turns blue when selected.

2

Describe Your Topic

In the large text box, type a brief description of what you need. Provide the subject, grade level, topic, and any specific objectives.

3

Paste Your Key & Generate

Paste the API Key you copied earlier into the designated box, then click "Generate Document". The AI will take roughly 10-15 seconds to build your file.

4

Refine and Download

On the preview screen, you can chat with the AI using the right sidebar to make specific changes (e.g., "Make the learning objectives shorter"). When satisfied, click "Download as Word".

warning

3. Important Document Notices

What does "---" mean?

When reviewing your downloaded Microsoft Word document, you will occasionally see the symbol "---" placed in areas like Principal names, specific dates, or supervisor signatures.

This is completely normal. "---" indicates a blank space where you must manually enter your specific school information. Because EduAssist AI does not know who your Principal or District Supervisor is, it intentionally leaves these fields blank for you to fill out manually outside of the scope of the app.
